A one-click SEO & AEO check for Webflow developers — run it right after finishing a page, before it goes live. Runs entirely in your browser, no install, no account.

Step 1 — drag this to your bookmarks bar

🔍 SEO Page Auditor

Clicking it here does nothing — you have to drag it up into your bookmarks bar.

  1. Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (⌘+Shift+B on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows/Linux).
  2. Drag the blue button above straight into that bar.
  3. Open any page you're working on, click the bookmark, and the audit panel appears.
Drag & drop not working? (trackpads, some browsers)
  1. Right-click the blue button above → Copy Link.
  2. Right-click your bookmarks bar → Add page… / Add bookmark.
  3. Name it "SEO Page Auditor", paste the copied link into the URL field, save.

Works on any site, not just Webflow — Webflow-specific checks simply won't trigger elsewhere.
